## Choose the Right Setup Before Ordering

Before purchasing a large quantity, look at the products that will actually occupy the bins. Small hardware may need compact compartments, while larger components may require deeper or wider containers. Weight also matters because the storage format should be appropriate for the material being placed inside.

Shelf dimensions should be measured as well. A bin that fits comfortably on one rack may not work on another. Taking these details into account helps create a storage arrangement that is useful from day one rather than requiring multiple adjustments after installation.

### Planning Checklist

* Measure available shelf space
* Identify the products to be stored
* Estimate typical inventory quantities
* Review product weight
* Separate high-use and reserve stock
* Decide on labeling requirements
* Determine the number of storage locations
* Allow room for future changes
